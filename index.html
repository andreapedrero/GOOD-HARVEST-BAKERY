Welcome to Conscious Baking

At Good Harvest Bakery, we handcraft delicious treats designed for health-conscious consumers and individuals with dietary restrictions. We use only organic, unrefined ingredients to guarantee pure flavor without the sugar crash.

Our Baking Standards

  • 100% Plant-Based: Egg-free and dairy-free alternatives that keep desserts fluffy and rich.
  • No Refined Sugars: Sweetened naturally with coconut sugar, dates, or pure organic maple syrup.
  • Gluten-Free Flour Selection: Safe, certified gluten-free options milled carefully to avoid cross-contamination.
