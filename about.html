How We Started

Good Harvest Bakery was founded out of a simple need: finding nutritious treats that satisfy a sweet tooth without compromising wellness goals. Our founder started experimenting with alternative flours and organic sweeteners in a home kitchen, eventually building a storefront for our local community.
